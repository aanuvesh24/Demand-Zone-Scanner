BIGBELUGA VOLUME-WEIGHTED ORDER BLOCK STRATEGY

THE BIGBELUGA QUANTITATIVE ENGINE DETECTS HIGH-PROBABILITY INSTITUTIONAL ACCUMULATION ZONES WHERE SMART MONEY ENTERED AGGRESSIVE LONG POSITIONS ON 15-MINUTE INTRADAY CHARTS.

01

SWING LOW PIVOT

LOCATES LOCAL SWING LOW PIVOT WHERE PRICE IS LOWER THAN SW_LEN BARS TO BOTH LEFT AND RIGHT.

02

DISPLACEMENT CHECK

REQUIRES EXPLOSIVE THRUST: CLOSENEXT - LOWPIVOT ≥ MULTIPLIER × ATR(14).

03

VOLUME CONVICTION

VERIFIES AGGRESSIVE BUYING: BUY_RATIO = (CLOSE - LOW) / (HIGH - LOW) ≥ 60% WITH VOLUME ≥ SMA10.

04

DEMAND ZONE BOUNDS

ZONE IS FORMED BETWEEN BOTTOM = LOWPIVOT AND TOP = MAX(OPENPIVOT, CLOSEPIVOT).

05

ACTIVE RETEST TRIGGER

FLAGS PRIME ENTRIES WHEN PRICE PULLS BACK INSIDE THE BOUNDARY: [0.998 × BOTTOM, 1.005 × TOP].

06

RISK / REWARD TARGETS

DEFINES RISK = TOP - BOTTOM, WITH AUTOMATED TARGET 2R AND TARGET 3R PROJECTIONS ABOVE THE ZONE.
